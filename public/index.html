What to eat

  • Biryani — Sindhi and Karachi styles, rich with spice
  • Nihari — slow-cooked beef stew, a Lahori breakfast
  • Chapli kebab — Peshawar’s crisp, juicy kebab
  • Sajji — Balochi roast lamb or chicken
  • Mangoes — Sindhri and Chaunsa, best in summer

Festivals and days

  • 23 March — Pakistan Day
  • Mid-May — Chilam Joshi, Kalash spring festival
  • Early July — Shandur Polo Festival, on one of the world’s highest polo grounds
  • 14 August — Independence Day
  • Eid ul-Fitr and Eid ul-Adha — dates follow the lunar calendar
